The lamp beside me emits a light at such an angle that I feel distressed. I will turn it off, and power on the portable mushroom-lamp. Portabella lamp. Heh. Well now, as I lie here, I'm pent up with unsorted thoughts and felt the call to write something here. I'll expound on paper later. I'll start by listing some things of import lately:
* I have been excessively focused on both accounting studies and programming.
* Those activities have taken over my life to such a degree that I chose them over much else and become anxious when occupied elsewhere.
* I accept that this season of life is dedicated to progress in those domains.
* I do not desire to continue this lifestyle indefinitely.
* I am not in the best health lately, having been very sedentary, mostly screen-facing, and prone to illness.
* I have been lax in my practices such as journaling, reflection, and reading.
* I have been struggling to achieve peace of mind and mental wellbeing, often compromising my quality and duration of sleep.
I have been unsettled by familial events that unfolded lately.
* I have become dejected at the idea of continuing my current job assignment for another year.
* I now regularly apply to alternative positions that will provide experience for my new career.
* My age again upticked and I feel somewhat stirred with urgency to escape this unrelated job.
* I am engrossed by shame when pondering my use of leave for personal and familial illness in spite of its legitimacy.
* I completed 4 of 5 tests for the last 4-credit accounting course; I'm on track.
* The course has been highly demanding of my time, moreso than ever before. In fact, I believe I have never invested this much time into a course.
* Due to the demand, I am pressured and naturally that presents an impediment to peace of mind.
* I have been leveraging programming to augment my accounting studies, but often I'd digress and pursue novel ideas and projects in technology.
* I had configured web hosting and VPS services and can now develop multiple sites concurrently.
* I have a long drive ahead of me, and I must return to work on Friday.
* I must develop a sort of plan to breathe some more structure and routine into my life, because the excessive screen-facing, despite the benefits in learning, are to the detriment of other life facets.
* I should also make a plan for 2026.
* I have been spending time with some good company, but can't help but feel detached and contently blasé. Any emotive expression is inhibited now more than ever.
* Old friends and cherished companions are still long gone.
* It seems this lifestyle is causing increasingly avoidant and awkward tendencies.
  I believe that my peace is disrupted recently primarily due to the coursework and secondarily due to workplace dissatisfaction. I just imagined for a brief moment no longer having the obligation to complete coursework and I felt quite at ease doing so. I'm almost there; relax. In the present, all is quiet, all is safe. I am secure.
  Though I mentioned wanting to create potential 2026 plans, I need to afford myself some respite and time away from this computer. I have gotten very ahead again with my coursework and I have programmed plenty. I can afford to focus my energy elsewhere for over a week ahead; perhaps I could muster a renewed enthusiasm for my current role and discard the frustrations for a time. Well, 2026 ideas for next time, and perhaps then I'll recap some experiences in Raleigh. Cheers,
