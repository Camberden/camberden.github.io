The day had come: my last day in uniform. I worked the shift as per usual and spent plentiful time with my fellow coworkers. At many times I had been emotionally moved; the significance of the figurative page turn is replete with meaning in this journey of life. I had received far more than I believe I would deserve, to include the opportunity to have been part of that team and to live in this area again, the pleasure of having had numerous stellar coworkers and trainees from whom I've learned plenty about life and the profession, and the send-off occassion itself during which coworkers had provided copious food and my supervisor had presented me a signed framing of my professional photo. It definitely shook me a little, and felt as if it signified a passing of a former self. After all, I am turning a page in life, and the contents to follow will reflect decoupling uniform and identity. I will afford myself the grace to adjust to this newness. In any case, thank you all: sergeant, corporal, officers, my trainees, and administration. Thank you.
