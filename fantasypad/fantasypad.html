Shown here is the map of the simply named "Great Regions," a Minecraft world born in May of 2012 whose lore and prospect for adventure and creation had not yet ceased. This world was the original creative sandbox for me, and I had invested plentiful hours into its development and maintenance. There are numerous regions (like countries), and cities with histories tied to their utility during the earliest era and imagined happenings beyond. The world has endured drastic world-edit transformations following the "Encroachment," a world event causing dramatic landscape alterations in late 2013. Since then, many are removed, but the world yet proves unweildy for a sole individual to flesh out to any degree of completion given its size.
